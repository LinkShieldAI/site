How it works

Send a URL. Get a clear result.

The goal is simple: help your team decide what should be allowed, blocked, or reviewed.

Submit the link

Send any URL you want to check, such as a user-submitted link or a link found during an investigation.

Review the result

Receive JSON fields such as result, screenshot URL, tag, probability, detection method, or matched signatures depending on the endpoint.

Use it in your workflow

Block risky links, hold uncertain ones for review, or add the result to your own tools.
