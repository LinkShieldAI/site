Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LinkShieldAI collects, uses, stores, and shares information across the LinkShieldAI website, Discord bot, developer portal, API, SDKs, browser extension projects, documentation, and related software.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Short version: LinkShieldAI is built to detect and prevent phishing. We process the minimum operational data we need to scan links, run the service, prevent abuse, support users, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

1. Scope

This policy applies when you visit LinkShieldAI websites, use the Discord bot, call the API, use developer tools, install or test LinkShieldAI software, access documentation, or otherwise interact with LinkShieldAI services.

2. Information We Process

Discord bot data

  • Messages that contain links, so the bot can inspect those links for phishing, malware, suspicious redirects, or abuse.
  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, role/configuration data, allowlists, blocklists, and moderation/action logs when needed for bot functionality.
  • Basic usage statistics, error logs, and operational diagnostics.

API, developer portal, and SDK data

  • Submitted URLs, domains, redirect information, threat verdicts, request timestamps, API keys, account identifiers, usage counts, and rate-limit events.
  • Billing status and subscription metadata if you use a paid plan.
  • Technical data such as IP address, user agent, error logs, and security events when needed to operate, secure, or troubleshoot the service.

Website and documentation data

  • Basic analytics or server logs, such as pages visited, approximate device/browser information, referral data, and error diagnostics.
  • Information you submit through contact, support, or developer portal forms.

3. How We Use Information

  • To scan links and return phishing, malware, reputation, or safety verdicts.
  • To provide Discord moderation features, alerts, configuration, allowlists, blocklists, and reports.
  • To operate the developer portal, authenticate API requests, enforce rate limits, and prevent abuse.
  • To improve detection quality, reliability, documentation, and user experience.
  • To investigate security incidents, spam, misuse, chargebacks, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To provide support, billing, account notices, and service updates.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when reasonably necessary for:

  • Hosting, infrastructure, security, logging, analytics, payment, support, or other service providers that help us operate LinkShieldAI.
  • Abuse prevention, public safety, malware/phishing investigation, blacklist management, or public reports where appropriate.
  • Legal compliance, law enforcement requests, dispute resolution, chargeback handling, or protection of LinkShieldAI, users, and the public.
  • A business transfer, merger, acquisition, or restructuring, subject to reasonable privacy safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as needed for service functionality, security, abuse prevention, debugging, analytics, billing, legal obligations, or legitimate operational needs. Some scan, log, or moderation records may be kept longer where they are necessary to prevent repeated abuse, support threat intelligence, or maintain account history.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect operational and account data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • Server admins can remove the Discord bot from a server or adjust bot configuration where available.
  • API users can rotate or revoke API keys through the developer portal when supported.
  • You may contact support to request access, correction, deletion, or review of account-related data, subject to security, legal, and operational limits.

8. Children's Privacy

LinkShieldAI is not intended to collect personal information from children. Discord users must comply with Discord's age requirements. If you believe a child provided personal information directly to LinkShieldAI, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International Use

LinkShieldAI may process information in countries where we or our providers operate. By using the service, you understand that information may be transferred and processed outside your location.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LinkShieldAI changes. When we make updates, we will revise the date on this page and may provide additional notice for material changes.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact LinkShieldAI through the Discord support server, the developer portal, or the contact channel listed in our documentation.
