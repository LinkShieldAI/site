Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how LinkShieldAI handles subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, API usage, billing errors, and chargebacks for paid services.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Our goal is to be clear and fair while keeping the API and security infrastructure sustainable. By using a paid LinkShieldAI service, you agree to this policy.

1. Subscription and Cancellation

  • Cancel anytime: You can cancel your subscription through dashboard settings where available, or by contacting support.
  • Effective date: After cancellation, your subscription normally remains active until the end of the current billing cycle, then it will not renew.
  • Data and API keys: We do not immediately delete API keys, account records, or operational data upon cancellation, but paid access may be restricted when the billing period ends.
  • Plan changes: Upgrades, downgrades, or usage-limit changes may take effect immediately or at the next billing cycle depending on the plan and billing provider.

2. General Refund Policy

To keep billing simple, LinkShieldAI generally follows a no-refunds policy for active API usage and subscription periods, with common-sense exceptions listed below.

  • Monthly plans: Monthly subscriptions are generally non-refundable once the billing period has started.
  • No partial refunds: We do not provide prorated refunds or credits for partial months of service or unused API calls.
  • Unused credits: Unused API calls, credits, or quota do not roll over and are not refundable unless your plan explicitly says otherwise.
  • Annual plans: For annual plans, you may request a refund within 7 days of the initial purchase or renewal, provided your API usage has not exceeded free-tier limits during that period.

3. Fairness Exceptions

We may issue a full or partial refund, credit, or other reasonable remedy in the following situations:

  • Technical failures: A confirmed LinkShieldAI bug or prolonged outage, outside scheduled maintenance, prevented meaningful use of the service for more than 24 consecutive hours.
  • Billing errors: You were incorrectly charged due to a clerical, payment, or system error on our part.
  • Duplicate charges: You were double-billed for the same account, period, or plan.
  • Accidental renewal: You contact support within 48 hours of renewal and the account has not materially used the paid service after renewal.

4. Chargebacks

Please contact support before initiating a chargeback. We can usually resolve billing issues faster than a bank dispute. Accounts with active chargebacks may be suspended or permanently terminated to prevent further unauthorized or disputed usage.

6. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you qualify for an exception, contact support with your account email, billing details, relevant dates, and the reason for your request. We aim to review refund requests within 3-5 business days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as LinkShieldAI plans, billing systems, or services evolve. The updated date on this page will show the latest version.
